Honest limitations

  • No CAPTCHA, login, paywall, or DRM bypass. If a destination needs a human to sign in or solve a challenge, LINKBREAK reports it instead of forcing its way in.
  • JavaScript-only challenges and ad-gates need manual completion. Services such as Linkvertise and Adf.ly hide the destination behind an interstitial ad page and a JavaScript challenge — those are reported as Unsupported, never resolved by trickery.
  • Not every short link resolves. Deleted links, expired links, and destinations that block automated requests will come back as Partially Resolved or Failed, with the reason shown.
  • Local and private destinations are always blocked (localhost, 192.168.x.x, 10.x.x.x, and similar) to prevent server-side request forgery.
  • Check the final domain before you click. Every result shows the final domain, flags a brand mismatch (for example a link that looks like a brand short link but lands elsewhere), and lists the full redirect chain — read the chain first, then decide whether to open the destination.
